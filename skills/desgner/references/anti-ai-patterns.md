# Anti-AI Pattern Catalog

These patterns are not universally forbidden. They fail when used by reflex rather than product/brand intent.

## Generic defaults to challenge

1. Purple/blue gradient dependence.
2. Gradient headline word.
3. Glassmorphism used as the default surface.
4. Everything rendered as a rounded card.
5. Radius inflation (`rounded-2xl/3xl` everywhere).
6. SaaS hero autopilot: badge + giant centered headline + two CTAs + mock dashboard.
7. Repeated icon + title + paragraph feature grids for unrelated content.
8. Decorative orbs/blobs/neon with no product meaning.
9. Generic “AI sparkle” iconography.
10. Every section centered.
11. Excess fades/slides on every element.
12. Theme inversion instead of semantic dark-mode mapping.
13. Fake stats, customer logos or testimonials.
14. Generic copy such as “unlock the power”, “revolutionize” or “seamless experience”.

## Correction principle

Prefer product specificity, semantic hierarchy, real content, restrained tokens, deliberate imagery, repeated spacing rhythm and meaningful exceptions.
